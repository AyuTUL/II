Group:
consider a binary strucutre (G,*). Set G is said to be a group
under binary soperation * if it follows following conditions:
Closure property:
for all a,b ∈ G, a*b ∈ G
Associative property:
for all a,b,c ∈ G, (a*b)*c=a*(b*c)
Existence of identity:
for all a∈G there exists idenity element e∈G such that a*e=e*a=a
Existence of inverse
for all a∈G there exists ai∈G such that a*ai=ai*a=e
